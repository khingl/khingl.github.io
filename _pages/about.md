I am a Teaching Assistant Professor in the [John Chambers College of Business and Economics](https://business.wvu.edu/) at West Virginia University, and an affiliate of the [Kendrick Center for an Ethical Economy](https://business.wvu.edu/research-outreach/kendrick-center-for-an-ethical-economy).

I study institutions and the industrial organization of science.

I completed my PhD in Economics at George Mason University under [Pete Boettke](https://www.peter-boettke.com/) and with [Tyler Cowen](https://tylercowen.com/), [Alex Tabarrok](https://alextabarrok.com/), and [Jonathan Schulz](https://jonathanfschulz.com/). I was also a PhD fellow with the Mercatus Center and a Graduate Fellow in the F.A. Hayek Program for Advanced Study in PPE.

Off the clock, I'm chasing my son Niko around with my wife Emily, ideally outside.
